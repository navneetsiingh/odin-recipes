Pasta Recipe

Description

Pasta is a delicious and versatile dish that can be customized with various sauces and toppings. This recipe makes a simple and tasty pasta dish that is perfect for a quick meal.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil leaves for garnish

Steps

  • Step1: Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  • Step2: Heat olive oil in a pan.
  • Step3: Add chopped onion and cook until soft.
  • Step4: Add minced garlic and cook for another minute.
  • Step5: Add tomato sauce and dried oregano.
  • Step6: Simmer for 10 minutes.
  • Step7: Drain the cooked pasta and mix with the sauce.
  • Step8: Garnish with fresh basil leaves.
