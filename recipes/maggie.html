Maggie Recipe

Description

Maggi is a quick and easy noodle recipe. It is one of the most popular snacks in India and can be made in just a few minutes.

This recipe uses Maggi noodles, water, masala, and a few vegetables to make it more tasty and filling.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles
  • 1 maggie masala packet
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 small tomato, chopped
  • 1 green chilli, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt as needed

Steps

  • Step1: Heat oil in a pan.
  • Step2: Add chopped onion and green chilli.
  • Step3: Cook for 1-2 minutes until the onion becomes soft.
  • Step4: Add chopped tomato and cook for another minute.
  • Step5: Add 1 cup of water and let it boil.
  • Step6: Add Maggi masala and mix well.
  • Step7: Add Maggi noodles.
  • Step8: Cook for 2-3 minutes until the noodles become soft.
  • Step9: Mix everything properly.
  • Step10: Serve hot.
